How the board is priced

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × size factor × momentum × maturity × breadth

Peer multiple

The median market-cap / TVL of the snapshot's own tokened protocols in the same category — ratios winsorised at the set's 95th percentile, thin categories shrunk toward the global median (—) with weight n/(n+6). No names are consulted; the yardstick is fitted from the data.

Size factor

Big protocols trade at lower multiples. A least-squares fit of log10(mcap/TVL) on log10(TVL) over the tokened set (slope —) is applied relative to a $100M reference protocol, capped between 0.35× and 1.60×.

Momentum

1 + 0.85 × the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped: growth is rewarded up to about 2.1×, shrinkage drags to no worse than 0.62×. Unreported momentum is neutral and lowers confidence instead.

Maturity

Rises with the days a protocol has run with no token recorded, from 0.72× toward 1.00× over roughly three years: a long pre-token track record supports a launch valuation.

Breadth & confidence

1.5% per extra chain, capped at eight. Every row also carries a confidence (calibration peers, reported momentum, known age, size) and a value range that widens as confidence falls.
