How the value is scored

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth

The category multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of this snapshot's own tokened protocols in the same category. Momentum is the 7-day TVL change at full weight, clamped to −50%..+150%. Maturity is log-scaled age since listing with no token. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain. Nothing is scored from a list of names: every figure comes from the sealed snapshot. Protocols whose token already trades sit apart in the Live view, at the market's own values, never mixed into the ranking.
